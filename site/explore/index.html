Where the boats are

Every ship at sea carries a radio that says, every few seconds, where it is. Denmark publishes all of it, small boats included, and I have been counting what it heard. This is the count on a map: pick a fleet, drag the month, or press play.

Each hexagon is about the size of a town, and the stronger its colour, the more boats of that fleet were out there that month. Small boats get bigger hexagons, about the size of a small island — coarse on purpose, so that no single boat can be picked out of one.

The slider only offers the months I actually loaded: the years 2015, 2018 and 2021, everything from spring 2024 to today, and a handful of single months around big storms. The years in between are missing from this map because I never loaded them, not because the sea was empty.
